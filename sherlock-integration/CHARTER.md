# Faraday Sherlock Bridge Charter

Preserve research source material, source-derived assertions, unresolved
contradictions, provenance gaps, and review questions that help evaluate Faraday
work without becoming Faraday scientific state.

Sherlock may reference Faraday inquiry, claim, protocol, run, evidence, and
synthesis identifiers as external records. Sherlock must not edit Faraday state,
replace Faraday admission gates, promote Faraday conclusions, or treat a
Sherlock annotation as scientific evidence.

Faraday remains the canonical owner of scientific protocols, datasets, runs,
evidence, claim ceilings, rigor audits, and deterministic synthesis. The bridge
records translation receipts only: which Faraday immutable summary was shown to
Sherlock, which Sherlock artifact or assertion refers to it, and which authority
boundary applies.

| ID | Question | What would change the assessment? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q01 | Which source materials or literature assertions should be preserved outside Faraday canonical state? | A reviewed source inventory shows that the material is already preserved under the appropriate Faraday artifact boundary or is outside the approved local workspace. |
| Q02 | Which unresolved contradictions, provenance gaps, or review questions should be tracked for later Faraday review? | A Faraday rigor audit, synthesis, or decision owner resolves the gap or shows it is irrelevant to the current inquiry scope. |
| Q03 | Which Faraday protocol, run, evidence, claim, or synthesis receipts have been exported for investigative inspection? | The referenced Faraday receipt hash changes, fails verification, is withdrawn, or is superseded by a new explicit export. |
